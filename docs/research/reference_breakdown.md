1. **The Tactical Revelation:** Viewers do not merely want to see moves; they want to feel the psychological dread of the opponent when an impossible move lands.
2. **Deterministic Quality:** Unlike random AI video generation which suffers from character instability and hallucinated chess rules, *Legends of the 64* ties every single frame to verifiable chess logic (legal FENs, accurate Stockfish evaluations) decorated with deterministic RPG cinematics.
