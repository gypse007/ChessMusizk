# Legends of the 64 — RPG Universe Bible

## 1. Lore & Cosmology
The realm known as **The 64 Grid** is an ancient astral dungeon constructed from 64 alternating obsidian and marble monoliths. Each square represents an elemental planar nexus.

In this universe:
- **Kings** are the Core Spirits whose defeat causes the entire pocket dimension to implode.
- **Queens** are Arch-Sorceresses possessing omni-directional devastation.
- **Rooks** are Living Siege Towers that grind forward across rank and file.
- **Bishops** are Zealot Inquisitors slicing diagonally across light or dark dimensions.
- **Knights** are Spectral Phantoms that defy planar geometry, leaping through obstacles.
- **Pawns** are Mortal Infantry whose final step across the 8th plane transforms them into Divine Ascendants.

## 2. Character Archetypes & Mechanics
1. **The Chaos Strategist (Mikhail Tal):** Channels dark arcane energy by offering pieces to overload the opponent's sensory matrix.
2. **The Dominator (Garry Kasparov):** Wields the Iron Attack; relentless tempo pressure, crushing space advantages.
3. **The Precision Strategist (José Raúl Capablanca):** Crystal clarity; strips complexity until only the pure endgame remains.
4. **The Precision Assassin (Bobby Fischer):** Absolute calculation; finding the "Only Move" in lethal tactical labyrinths.
5. **The Rapid Striker (Paul Morphy):** Overwhelming development and open files; punishes sluggish preparation instantly.
6. **The Defensive Master (Tigran Petrosian):** Prophetic prophylaxis; neutralizing enemy ambitions before they manifest.

## 3. Visual & Aesthetic Guidelines
- **Atmosphere:** Dark fantasy esports. Volumetric dust, ancient runes carved into marble floor tiles, ethereal glowing piece projections.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Background: Deep Charcoal (`#09090b`), Dark Slate (`#18181b`)
  - Highlight/Aura Colors: Violet Void (`#8b5cf6`), Blood Crimson (`#ef4444`), Royal Gold (`#eab308`), Electric Cyan (`#06b6d4`)
- **Typography:** High-impact gothic/cinematic headers paired with clean geometric monospace stats for damage, evaluation swings, and move notations.
